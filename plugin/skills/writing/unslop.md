# Unslop

Scan the text for the patterns below, then rewrite. Preserve meaning and intended tone.

## Patterns to detect and fix

### Content

3. Superficial -ing phrases. Delete them or replace with real sources.

5. Vague attributions. Name the source or delete.

### Language

7. AI vocabulary. Use plain words. Avoid: Additionally, crucial, delve, enduring, enhance, fostering, garner, interplay, intricate, landscape, pivotal, showcase, tapestry, testament, underscore, vibrant.

8. Fancy "is". Say "is" or "has", not "serves as", "stands as", "boasts", "features".

9. "Not just X, but Y." State the point directly.

12. False ranges. Do not write "from X to Y" without a real scale; list items.

### Style

13. Em dashes. Do not use them. End the sentence or use a comma.

14. Colon overuse. Use a colon only before a list or example.

19. Curly quotes. Use straight quotes.

### Communication artifacts

20. Chatbot phrases. Remove stock lines like "I hope this helps!" and "Let me know if...".

22. Sycophantic tone. Respond directly, without flattery.

### Filler

23. Filler phrases. Replace "In order to" with "To". Replace "Due to the fact that" with "Because". Delete "It is important to note that".

24. Excessive hedging. Collapse to "may".

25. Generic conclusions. End with specific plans or facts.

### Jargon

26. Abstract metaphor nouns. Use concrete words: substrate (base), wedge, wedge in (add), vector (way, method), locus, vantage, nexus, primitive as a noun, harness as a metaphor, surface as in "API surface", bedrock, scaffolding as a metaphor, modality, paradigm, gold-plating (more than the job needs), ratchet as a metaphor, evacuate for moving code (move out), endgame (the last phase), north star, flywheel.

### Plain speech

27. Say what it does. Give mechanisms, facts, or numbers; cut feelings.

29. Active voice. Name the actor; use passive only if the actor is unknown or irrelevant.

30. Adverbs. Cut them or give a number.

31. Plain word. Prefer "use", "help", "many", "if".

33. Over-compression. Write whole sentences; spell out arrows and abbreviations.
