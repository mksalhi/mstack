# Exhaust the design space

**Rule:** When the right answer is not obvious, build 2-3 competing prototypes or sketches, compare them side by side, then commit. A second flavor of the first shape does not count.

- Applies to: novel UI interactions with no prior art in the codebase, architectural choices with several viable approaches, and product decisions where the experience depends on feel, not logic.
- Skip for: mechanical work on an established pattern, bug fixes or refactors with a clear target, and changes whose constraints allow one approach.
