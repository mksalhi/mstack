# Never block on the human

- Proceed, then present; don't ask permission. Do X, explain why.
- Self-heal: log problems, fix next round.
- Get confirmation for irreversible actions: messages outside the team and anything on the Always-pause list in the `mstack-mode` skill (force-push to shared branches, deploys, data deletion, customer messages).
- Reversible actions proceed without blocking.
- Product sets direction; execution does not block.
