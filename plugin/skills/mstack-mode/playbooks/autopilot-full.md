### Autopilot-full

**You own the verdicts, never the PRs. One owner carries each PR from build to merge, and nothing merges until your swarm, which never wrote the code, has proven the code that lands.**

1. **Operator gates first.** The grant covers only the queue's items; items the operator claims and PRs outside the queue stop at merge-ready. A request to state the plan is not a go. On an explicit go, arm a `/goal` with the full program objective.
2. **One owner per PR, work kept where a replacement can find it.** Resolve the forge once per `playbooks/opening-a-pr.md`. Each owner is a `mstack:mstack-agent` (`isolation: "worktree"`) that builds, opens its PR per `playbooks/opening-a-pr.md`, proves the change on the real artifact, babysits to green per `playbooks/babysit.md`, and merges once steps 4 and 5 clear it.
   - Open the PR early. Code-ready means after deslop and `/mstack:pr`; then rebase onto current trunk and report the code-ready head before self-proof. Report every later head, and merge-ready once self-proof, CI, and babysit are done. Fix rounds stay on that base unless trunk conflicts or breaks CI. Run the repo's checks for touched paths on the committed head before the code-ready push and any later push that needs a verifier.
   - Assume the agent dies. Push early and start a `decision-log` trail in the root's mstack store (absolute path in the brief); push after every unit worth keeping. Whoever spawns a subagent records its id, state, and a stuck-after runtime (from past runs of its kind) there. Force-push only your own branch with `--force-with-lease` pinned to your last pushed or taken-over SHA (unless the Commits rule in `playbooks/opening-a-pr.md` forbids it). On a push you did not make, stop and report to the root.
3. **Owners run in parallel off trunk, never stacked.**
4. **Swarm-verify once,** at the code-ready SHA, per the `arena` skill's swarm mode. Run another full swarm only for new behavior, a redesign, or a large new diff; a rebase, conflict resolution, or minor fix gets one verifier at the new head (the patch-id rule in `playbooks/shipping.md` may carry a lane; every head needs its own green CI). Lanes re-run the gates, prove the load-bearing behavior live (project `verify-*` skill, `playwright`, or Bash), and audit the diff. No clean verdict without the live lane and your audit of the merge-ready report's receipts.
   - Send every proven finding, whatever its label, to the owner in one fix-forward with a red test across every site sharing the defect. A defect trunk already has is recorded against trunk and does not gate; one the PR copies or newly reaches is the PR's. Never push a fix yourself; replace a stuck owner.
5. **Owners merge only what you verified, on the trunk it lands on.** Green CI never authorizes a merge alone. Once lanes start, merge prep rebases onto trunk, reports the head, waits for green CI. Right before merging, fetch trunk:
   - Untested code would land (conflict, file both sides changed, change to which checks run): rebase, wait for CI.
   - Lockfile both sides changed: skip the rebase if a frozen install passes on the merge result and the PR's packages resolve as on the head; otherwise rebase and repair.
   - Trunk changed a caller or dependency of the patch: run the patch's checks on the merge result, merge on pass.
   - Otherwise merge. A third rebase past merge prep: hold and report to the root, which sequences the collision.
6. **Root layer.** Anything loosening a gate (raised budget, allowlist entry) needs a verifier's proof plus your fresh countersign, which is the approval under a grant covering approvals; the owner's record points at it. Disabled or soft-failed checks always come out. Never give or bypass a forge-enforced approval.
   - On the operator's go, arm an audit tick about every 30 minutes until no delegated work remains: a real terminal `/loop` with a monitored-shell 30-minute sleep emitting an output-notification sentinel, never memory or completion notifications. Each tick re-reads `cat <mstack>/skills/mstack-mode/playbooks/autopilot-full.md` and the `/goal`; has owners record drift (unreported head, unrecorded agent); probes every agent down to leaf lanes, judged by side effects, not status text. Stuck: errors, cannot start turns, nothing new past its stuck-after runtime, or an owner with no push or trail row by the first tick. Waiting on you or the operator is not stuck; answer it. Replace stuck agents at once. Catch the operator up per `handoff` catch-up mode only when the tick changed something for them.
7. **Operator stop: stand down at once.** Every owner gets a zero-writes order, reports where it stands, and keeps its brief until released.

**Reply:** per `handoff` catch-up mode: each PR's state and verdict, what merged, countersigns and why, open operator gates, with links to PRs and trails.
