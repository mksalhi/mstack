# Product film brief

## The ask

Make a [20]-second motion graphics film for [PRODUCT] ([URL]) with the energy of a motion designer's showreel.

## Assets

- Capture real screenshots, logo, colours and fonts from the site into `./assets`; list them before animating.
- Never redraw product UI from imagination. Crop and animate the captures.
- [Optional: a reference frame or video in `./refs`, and what to take from it.]

## Story: one beat each, 2 to 4 seconds

1. **Hook.** The problem, in about five words of very large type.
2. **Arrival.** The product appears and its UI assembles piece by piece.
3. **Three features.** Each is a UI moment in which a cursor performs a real action: [FEATURE 1], [FEATURE 2], [FEATURE 3].
4. **Proof.** One number that shows it works: [METRIC].
5. **Close.** Logo lockup and [CALL TO ACTION].

## Sound

- [Original music at 120 BPM, synthesized in code | the supplied track at `./audio/track.wav`, unchanged].
- UI clicks and whooshes on the beat.
- [Optional voice: a character who explains the product. The key is `[VOICE_KEY_NAME]` in `.env`.]

## Format

[9:16] first, then [1:1] and [16:9] from the same timeline, reframed and not cropped.

## Gate

Show the shot list on the beat grid and a draft contact sheet before the full render.

## Notes

Keep one working session per brand; later films reuse assets, engine and sound setup.
