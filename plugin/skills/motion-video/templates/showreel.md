# Showreel brief

For testing the engine or showing technique only; use a product or director's brief for anything real.

## The ask

Make a [15]-second motion graphics film in [9:16] that works as your own showreel: the reel a motion designer would put at the top of a portfolio. Every shot uses a different technique, the strongest shot comes first, and nothing repeats. Push it as far as you can.

## Pick one variation

- **With a score.** [60] seconds, [16:9]. Compose an original [piano] piece in code and cut every shot to it. No generic synth pads.
- **Anti-giveaway.** [10] seconds introducing who you are. No frames around the picture and no small text in the corners.
- **A story.** Keep the reel's energy but tell the history of [TOPIC] from [START] to today. [45] seconds, vertical. Propose the storyboard first.
- **A studio.** [30] seconds as the reel of a small branding studio for [AUDIENCE]. Every graphic is made from scratch, with one accent colour.

## Rules

- Follow the house rules in `AGENTS.md`.
- Sound is synthesized in code and lands on the beat grid.
- Show a draft contact sheet before the full render.
